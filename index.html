Why this is hard

Nobody has a map of how the work really moves

Work rarely follows the process chart

When a team is busy, people find their own way through. The official path becomes the exception, not the rule.

Most of it never gets written down

One matter bounces between email, your practice system, Teams, a phone call, Word, and back to email. That journey isn't in any document.

By the time you've written it down, it's changed

Ways of working drift the moment they're documented. The manual quietly goes out of date, and nobody notices until something goes wrong.

Our approach

We listen, scan and map — then show you where work is drifting

Four steps, most of which happen without your team lifting a finger. The map that comes out is specific to your firm — generated by AI from how your people really work together.

We start with your people.

Listen

Before anything is switched on, we sit with your team to hear how the work feels from the inside — where it snags, what gets worked around, what nobody has had time to write down. That shapes what we look for.

Lightweight AI watches the work, not the people.

Scan

A small observer notices how work moves between your systems — which tool, which hand-off, how long each step takes. There's nothing to integrate and nothing for your team to do. It runs quietly inside your own environment.

AI draws a map that's specific to your firm.

Map

Those observations are stitched into workflows, and the workflows into a living map of your firm — including the multiplayer ones that cross desks and departments. It keeps itself up to date as your practice changes.

See where work is drifting, before it costs you.

Predict

The map shows the usual path through each kind of matter, where work is starting to stray from it — and where it's likely to next. So you can act early, instead of finding out when the invoice lands.

Platform

A clear picture of your whole firm

Three parts, one living system — from quiet observation, to a map of your firm, to an early warning when work starts to drift.

Scan — a quiet observer

One lightweight observer sits inside your environment and notices how work moves across the systems your firm already uses. Nothing to wire up, nothing for your team to change, and it costs them no time.

  • Nothing to integrate — it works with what you have
  • Runs inside your environment, so data stays with you
  • Set up in days; invisible from day one

Map — your firm, drawn by AI

What the observer sees is turned into a map of how your firm actually operates: the departments, the workflows, the roles and tools that hold them together — and the hand-offs between them. It reshapes itself as every new observation arrives.

  • Every variation, exception and workaround, captured
  • Multiplayer workflows that cross desks and departments
  • Every part of the map traces back to something observed

Predict — an early warning on drift

The map shows the usual path through each kind of matter, how often work leaves it, and where it's likely to leave it next — so problems are caught while they're still small.

  • The usual path, and how often each turn is taken
  • Where work is straying, shown in proportion to how often
  • A marker where the next deviation is expected
